Principles
How Ronela designs tools
  • Make it fast: reduce steps, keep inputs simple, and show results immediately.
  • Make it legible: clear wording, consistent structure, and readable outputs.
  • Make it useful: focus on outcomes—checklists, drafts, plans, and next actions.
  • Make it human: AI drafts; humans refine; feedback improves the final feel.
